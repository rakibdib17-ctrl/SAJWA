# SAJWA Adhan audio attribution

The bundled Adhan recording is fetched at build time from Wikimedia Commons:

- File: [Adhan.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Adhan.ogg)
- Author: Aishatu98
- Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
- Source page records the author as the copyright holder and the recording as their own work.
- The audio is included unchanged in the Android APK as `res/raw/adhan.ogg`.

The source recording is approximately 42 seconds long. It is used as the general Adhan sound for all five prayer alarms. SAJWA does not currently bundle a separate Fajr recording.
